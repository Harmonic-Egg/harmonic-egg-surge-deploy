The Healing Power of Music: Discovering the Essence of Letting Go

Harmonic Egg Dispatch / Published October 15, 2026 / 3 min read

There are certain melodies that transcend time and space, allowing us to connect with our innermost feelings during times of loss and change. Music can help us express emotions that may be hard to articulate, providing solace and comfort on our healing journeys.

This week, we would like to share an original orchestral composition titled Letting Go, a beautiful collaboration between myself, Gail Lynn, and the talented composer Jeremy Thompson. This 40-minute piece invites listeners to explore their emotions while easing the burdens of grief.

The journey of creating Letting Go began when an ALS foundation reached out to me, requesting a piece of music to support those affected by amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. It was a request that resonated deeply with me. As I pondered the role of music, especially within the context of the Harmonic Egg®, I understood that sound could facilitate healing during life’s most challenging moments.

I was honored to work alongside Jeremy, who had his own personal connection to ALS through loss. Together, we poured our hearts into this composition, bringing Letting Go to life.

Music is especially powerful in times when words may fail us. Letting Go was originally inspired by the request for those grappling with ALS, but its message holds a universal truth about facing grief and loss of any kind.

Loss can be felt in many ways: the passing of a loved one, the ending of a relationship, shifts in our health, or even the closing of a significant chapter in life. At times, we find ourselves mourning changes in our surroundings that we never anticipated.

Reflecting the five stages of grief—Denial, Anger, Bargaining, Depression, and Acceptance—this musical experience is crafted to create a safe space for emotional exploration and release.

There’s no need to rush through your feelings or pretend to be fine before you truly are. Sometimes, taking a moment to simply listen can be the beginning of significant healing.

Interested in experiencing Letting Go? We invite you to take a moment to listen to a sample, allowing the orchestral harmonies to embrace your spirit and emotions. Music has a remarkable way of expressing what words cannot.
