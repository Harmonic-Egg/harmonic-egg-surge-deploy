Prioritizing Integrity in Sound: Inquire if Your Wellness Provider Holds a Commercial Music License. Your Inquiry Matters!

When you visit wellness centers, spas, or any therapeutic environments where music is played, it is beneficial to ask whether they maintain an active commercial music license.

Historically, music creators have missed out on significant royalties due to unauthorized public performances and evolving tech platforms.

Using personal accounts or unlicensed tracks in business settings deprives artists of just compensation.

This ongoing issue of fair payment diminishes the integrity of healing spaces, something we aspire to nourish. True therapeutic integrity relies on ethical business practices that honor the artistry behind the music used in healing.

By simply asking this pivotal question, you enhance the energy of healing spaces.
